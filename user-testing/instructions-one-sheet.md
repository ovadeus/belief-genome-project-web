# Belief Genome Project — User Testing Session Guide

Thank you for helping us refine the Belief Genome Project (BGP). This session is about your honest reactions, not getting anything "right." There are no wrong answers — we want to learn how the experience feels for you.

**Estimated time:** 45–60 minutes total. You can pause and resume at any point.

---

## What you'll do today

### 1. Read and sign the consent form  *(~3 min)*

Your researcher will hand you a consent form. Read it, ask any questions, then sign. The form explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your right to withdraw at any time.

### 2. Register for the BGP  *(~2 min)*

Open the link your researcher provides. Click **"Create one"** at the bottom of the sign-in screen. Fill in:

- Email (any working address)
- Password (something memorable but private)
- Birth year, month, day, and country — these become part of your "DNA" identity prefix and never leave your account

You'll be signed in automatically once registration completes.

### 3. Sign in  *(~1 min)*

If you ever get signed out, just go back to the same link and sign in with the email and password you chose.

### 4. Answer 100–200 probes  *(~20–35 min — the main event)*

This is the heart of the session. You'll see one statement at a time. For each, choose how true it feels for you, from "Absolute False" to "Absolute True," with "Uncertain" in the middle.

**A few important things to know:**

- **Answer with your gut, not your "best self."** The tool works best when you respond honestly, not how you think you should respond.
- **"Uncertain" is a real answer.** It's not a cop-out. Saying "I don't know" or "it depends" is meaningful data.
- **Probes come in pairs.** You may notice some probes feel like the opposite of one you saw earlier. That's intentional — it's how we measure consistency. Just answer each one fresh based on how it reads to you in the moment.
- **You don't have to finish in one sitting.** Your progress saves automatically. Aim for at least 100 probes today; 200 is the upper end if you have the energy.
- **It's okay to skip an uncomfortable probe** — but if you're consistently uncomfortable, please tell your researcher.

### 5. Explore your visualizations  *(~10 min)*

Once you've answered enough probes, navigate through the BGP visualizations. Look at:

- **Your DNA string** — a compact summary of your belief patterns, with colored characters representing your stance on each dimension.
- **The Triple Helix, Neuromap, and Evolution views** — different ways of seeing the same underlying data.
- **Your dashboard** — overall confidence, dimensions covered, and recent activity.

Take your time. Hover over things. Click around. Let yourself notice what you notice.

### 6. Complete the follow-up feedback form  *(~10 min)*

Your researcher will hand you (or email you) a short feedback form. Please fill it in while the experience is still fresh. Your honest impressions — including critical ones — are the most valuable thing you can give us.

---

## A few tips for getting the most out of this

- **Phone, tablet, or laptop all work.** Use whatever feels most natural to you.
- **Find a quiet spot if possible.** The probes ask you to look inward; distraction makes that harder.
- **Don't overthink.** First-instinct answers are the ones we're after.
- **Feel free to take breaks.** Step away, breathe, come back.

## Privacy

Your individual responses stay tied to your account and are visible only to you and the research team. We will not share your individual data outside this study. You can request deletion of your account and all responses at any time by emailing the researcher.

## If something goes wrong

- The page won't load → tell your researcher; they can refresh or try a different browser.
- You forgot your password → use the same registration page to reset, or ask the researcher to help.
- A probe seems broken or in the wrong language → make a quick mental note (or jot down the wording) so you can mention it in the feedback form.
