# Belief Genome Project — Session Guide

Welcome, and thank you for helping us. This guide walks you through everything you'll do today.

There are no right or wrong answers. We just want your honest reactions.

**Time needed:** about 45 to 60 minutes. You can pause at any time.

---

## What you will do today (6 steps)

1. **Read and sign the consent form.** Your researcher will hand it to you. Read it, ask any questions, then sign.

2. **Create your account.** Open the link your researcher gives you. Click "Create one" at the bottom of the sign-in screen. Fill in your email, a password, and your birth date and country.

3. **Sign in.** You will be signed in automatically after you register. If you ever get signed out, just go back to the same link and sign in again.

4. **Answer 100 to 200 questions.** This is the main part of the session, about 20 to 35 minutes. One question at a time. For each one, choose how true it feels for you, from "Absolute False" to "Absolute True." "Uncertain" is also a real answer.

5. **Look at your results.** Once you have answered enough questions, explore the visual pictures the tool draws of your beliefs. Take your time. Hover, click, and see what you notice.

6. **Fill out the feedback form.** Your researcher will hand you a short form. Please complete it while the experience is still fresh.

---

## Important things to know about the questions

1. **Answer with your gut.** First-instinct answers work best. Do not try to give the "right" answer.

2. **"Uncertain" is a real answer.** It is not a cop-out. Saying "I don't know" or "it depends" is meaningful.

3. **Some questions feel like opposites of earlier ones.** This is on purpose. It helps us check that the tool is working. Just answer each one fresh.

4. **You can pause and come back.** Your progress saves on its own. Aim for at least 100 questions today. 200 is the upper end.

5. **It is okay to skip a question that feels too uncomfortable.** If many questions feel uncomfortable, please tell your researcher.

---

## Tips for getting the most out of this

1. Use whatever device feels natural — phone, tablet, or laptop.

2. Find a quiet spot if you can. The questions ask you to look inward.

3. Do not overthink. Trust your first answer.

4. Take breaks when you need them.

---

## Your privacy

Your individual answers are tied to your account and can be seen only by you and the research team. We will not share your individual data outside this study. You can ask us to delete your account and all answers at any time by emailing the researcher.

---

## If something goes wrong

1. **The page will not load** — tell your researcher. They can refresh or try a different browser.

2. **You forgot your password** — use the same sign-in page to reset it, or ask the researcher.

3. **A question seems broken or in the wrong language** — make a note of it so you can mention it in the feedback form.
